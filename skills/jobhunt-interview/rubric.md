# Answer rubric

Score each criterion 1-4. Total out of 20. Quote the user's words as evidence.

| Criterion | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Answers the question** | Talks about something else | Partly on topic | On topic, some drift | Direct answer in the first two sentences |
| **Structure (STAR)** | No situation or no result | Situation and task, thin action, no result | All four parts, uneven | Clear situation, task, action, result in proportion (action is the longest) |
| **Ownership** | Only "we", own role unclear | Own role stated vaguely | Own decisions named | Own decisions, trade-offs and reasoning named |
| **Evidence** | No specifics | Specifics but no outcome | Outcome stated without measure | Outcome with a number, a before/after, or a concrete consequence |
| **Relevance and reflection** | No link to this job | Link implied | Link stated | Link stated plus what they learned or would do differently |

## Bands

- 17-20: ready. Polish delivery only.
- 13-16: solid. One criterion to fix.
- 9-12: the story is there but buried. Restructure.
- 5-8: pick a different story or gather the missing facts first.

## Common fixes

- No result: ask "What changed because of what you did? How did anyone know?"
- "We" answers: ask "What was your part, specifically? What did you decide?"
- Too long: cut the situation to two sentences; the interviewer cares about the action.
- No reflection: add one sentence: "Next time I would ..." or "Since then I ..."
